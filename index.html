故事The story

一月到十二月January to December

阿夏来新加坡当一年秘密美食评审。每个月，她挑一个星期五回到 Newton，给一个档口打分。头顶的挂饰从红灯笼换成新月、小旗和星星灯，一年就这么过去。到这里的第一个星期五，阿夏排完队回来，那张用纸巾占住的凳子上已经坐了阿杰。两人吵了一架，最后一人坐一半。阿杰家在这里卖沙爹，档主是他爸爸陈伯。阿姨们早听说今年有评审要来，在黑板上开了赌局：背大相机的游客一赔二，天天看报纸的林先生一赔四，从外地来、一个人吃三盘的阿夏一赔九。阿夏每次吃完，都在一本红笔记本上写几行。Summer has a year in Singapore as an undercover food critic. One Friday each month she returns to Newton to review a stall. Overhead, red lanterns give way to crescents, flags and Christmas stars. On her first Friday, Summer comes back from the queue to find Kelvin sitting on the stool she saved with her tissues. They argue, and end up sharing it. Kelvin’s family runs the satay stall here; his father, Uncle Tan, works the grill. The aunties have heard a critic is coming this year, and they open a book on the blackboard: the tourist with the big camera at two to one, Mr Lim with his newspaper at four to one, and Summer, from far away and eating three plates on her own, at nine to one. After every meal Summer writes a few lines in a red notebook.

六月，一家水獭冲进小贩中心，拖走了阿夏的包。红笔记本掉出来，赔率一赔九的那一注中了，大家这才知道她就是评审。九月中秋，阿杰提着灯笼请她吃豆花，求她给自家沙爹写一个差评。好评一出来，档口挂上推荐灯笼，队就会排起来，摊子关不掉，他也就走不成。In June a family of otters rushes into the hawker centre and drags off Summer’s bag. Her red notebook falls out. The long-shot bet, at one to nine, wins, and everyone sees that she is the critic. At the Mid-Autumn Festival, Kelvin brings her a lantern and a bowl of tau huay, and asks her to give the family satay a bad review. Once that review is public, the queue will start, the stall will be unable to close, and he will be unable to leave.

十二月最后一个星期五，轮到沙爹摊。陈伯其实第一天就认出了她。今晚他故意把沙爹烤咸，好让儿子能去伦敦。父子俩都瞒着对方，都在为对方放手。On the last Friday in December, it is the satay stall’s turn. Uncle Tan recognised her on the first day. Tonight he salts the satay on purpose, so his son can go to London. Father and son each hide this from the other, and each is trying to let the other go.

主意象
不画热闹的人群，只铺一整片雨后微湿的娘惹花砖，上面一张红塑料凳、一包斜放的纸巾，全部从正上方俯拍。东西越少越安静，热闹留给歌，不放进画面。
十二块地砖
十二格用的是同一块实拍釉面花砖（从 dishes/00-floor-4x3.jpg 里裁出来，铺成 dishes/00-floor-sym.jpg）：每格正中一朵完整的圆花，四角的小花在格子交界处拼成整朵，横竖都对称，上面只留一层很淡的午后斜光；第 6 格留着水獭的湿脚印。下面左边是阿夏的红笔记本和阿姨的黑板，一本私人、一块公开，放在一起看，本子上用胶带贴着这个月那道菜的照片；右边是剧情和双栏混语场刊。
纸面痕迹
红笔记本用硬笔手写体，分数用圆珠笔圈出来，纸面跟着剧情留下痕迹：二月划掉了瞎编的职业，四月页角被雨水晕开，六月沾了水獭脚印，十月溅上两点红辣油，十二月在最后一句底下重重画了线。
头顶的挂饰与光线
新加坡没有四季，靠头顶挂的东西记月份。石灰墙的底色也随十二个月一点点变暖，从雨后放晴的下午，走到年底炭火的暖光。右上角随时可以切到「夜市 21:00」。
人物
人物不画正脸，也不加卡片边框。五张铅笔背影用正片叠底直接印在石灰墙上（切到夜市时变成浅浅的粉笔线），每人带着自己的东西：纸巾和薄本子；背心、拖鞋和蒲扇；木炭盆；托盘上的咖啡壶和杯子；两大三小的水獭拖着帆布包，红本子从包里滑了出来。
双结局终曲
选「留下」或「回去」，会翻开最后一张座位卡，同时展开对应的镜像重唱《Stay》或《Go Home》。两条路最后都接到全员谢幕曲《Last Order》，十二个档口的厨房乐器一起响。

主图静物 prompt

The image
No crowd. One continuous floor of wet Peranakan tiles, one red stool from above, one crooked packet of tissues. The noise lives in the songs.
Twelve tiles
One real glazed Peranakan tile, cut from dishes/00-floor-4x3.jpg and laid twelve times as dishes/00-floor-sym.jpg: a whole medallion centred in every square, the corner rosettes meeting where the squares join, symmetrical both ways under a faint wash of afternoon light. June carries wet otter prints. Below, Summer’s private red notebook, with that month’s dish photo taped to it, and the aunties’ public chalkboard sit together beside the scene and bilingual libretto.
Paper traces
The exercise-book page carries hard-pen script, circled scores, and physical traces of the year: a crossed-out alibi in February, a rain-blurred corner in April, otter smudges in June, chilli flecks in October, and an underlined plea in December.
Garland and light
With no seasons, the year is counted by what hangs overhead, while the limewash wall shifts subtly through twelve afternoon tints toward December’s charcoal warmth. Switch to the 21:00 night market at top right.
People
No front-facing faces and no card borders. Five graphite back-views blend directly into the plaster wall (turning to chalk lines at night), each with its object: tissues and notebook, singlet and palm fan, charcoal basin, kopi tray, and two adult otters with three pups dragging off the canvas bag.
Two endings
Choosing Stay or Go Home opens both the final seat card and its mirror reprise (Stay or Go Home), before both paths converge in the full-company curtain call Last Order.

Key-art prompt

Leave the crowd. Shoot an overhead still life on wet Peranakan tiles — one red plastic stool and one crooked packet of tissues, answering the twelve ceramic floor tiles above.

Editorial still-life photograph, shot directly from above, 4:5 vertical.

A single red plastic hawker-centre stool, seen from straight overhead, standing on a floor of hand-painted Peranakan tiles in celadon green, cobalt blue, coral and mustard on an off-white ground. On the stool lies a small unopened packet of white tissues, placed slightly crooked, the way people in Singapore use it to save a seat. A tropical rainstorm has just ended: a few raindrops bead on the stool and the tiles, one fallen white frangipani flower lies near a stool leg, and the soft shadow of a palm frond falls across one corner. Late-afternoon light after rain, clear and gentle.

Lots of empty tile floor around the stool, the stool slightly below centre, quiet and generous negative space at the top for a title. Calm, elegant, cheerful, precise. Natural colours, fine film grain, shallow shadows.
